Privacy

This website gives information about Dr Mugdha Jungari and The Purple Women's Health Clinic. It does not ask you for any personal or medical information, and it has no forms or accounts.

What the website collects

The website itself does not set cookies or collect personal details. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Services from other companies

The map on the home page is provided by Google Maps. When it loads, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. Calling, WhatsApp, directions and Instagram links open other services, which handle your information under their own policies.

Your medical information

Please do not send medical reports or personal health details through social media. Bring them to your consultation, or ask the clinic how to share them.
