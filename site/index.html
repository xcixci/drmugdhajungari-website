About Dr Mugdha

Dr Mugdha Jungari has practised obstetrics and gynaecology for 19 years. Her work covers pregnancy from the first visit to recovery after birth, including pregnancies that need closer monitoring, and gynaecological care from adolescence through menopause.

She is trained in minimal access (keyhole) surgery. Alongside her practice she teaches, through postgraduate training and work on the NMC curriculum for future doctors.

She founded The Purple Women's Health Clinic in Pratap Nagar, where partners are welcome to join consultations.

Care through each stage

What Dr Mugdha treats, set out by the stage of life it usually comes up in.

  1. Adolescence

    Irregular or painful periods, PCOS, and questions about a changing body.

  2. Planning a pregnancy

    A check-up before you try, and help when PCOS or irregular cycles make conceiving harder.

  3. Pregnancy

    Antenatal care through each trimester, with closer monitoring for high-risk pregnancies.

  4. Birth and after

    Labour and delivery, recovery after birth, and BirthWise classes to prepare you both.

  5. Menopause

    Guidance through the hormonal and physical changes of perimenopause and menopause.

At any age

  • GynaecologyDiagnosis and treatment of gynaecological concerns, with care that fits each stage of life.
  • Laparoscopic surgeryKeyhole surgery through small incisions, as well as open surgery when it is needed.
  • NDVHNon-descent vaginal hysterectomy: removal of the uterus without a cut on the abdomen.

BirthWise antenatal classes

Classes that prepare you for pregnancy, childbirth and the first weeks of parenthood. Couples are encouraged to attend together, so both of you know what to expect and how to support each other through labour.

Ask at the clinic about the next classes, or follow BirthWise on Instagram.
